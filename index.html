One library for every source

Connect the places your music already lives. OKOMUSE scans them, reads tags and artwork, and builds a single library you can browse by artist, album, genre or folder.

Made for listening, not for managing

Everything you expect from a great player, plus the details that make long albums, audiobooks and podcasts a pleasure.

Stream or keep it offline

Play straight from the cloud, or download a track, a folder or a whole playlist for flights, subways and anywhere without signal. Recently played tracks are cached automatically, so they start instantly next time.

10-band equalizer

Shape your sound and save presets for headphones, car or speakers.

Book mode

Skip back and forward by 10 or 15 seconds — in the player and on the Lock Screen. Built for audiobooks and podcasts.

Pick up where you left off

OKOMUSE remembers your position and can drop a bookmark every time you pause.

Bookmarks

Mark any moment in a track — a chapter, a solo, a quote — and jump back to it later.

Playback speed

Listen faster or slower without losing your place. Perfect for lectures and long reads.

Gapless playback

Live albums and DJ mixes flow from one track to the next without a gap of silence.

Radio

An endless mix built from your own library. Press play and rediscover what you already own.

Playlists

Build mixes from any source and sync them through your own cloud storage.

Sleep timer

Fall asleep to your music. Playback stops on its own when the timer ends.

Find anything in seconds

Search your whole library by title, artist or album. Browse by folder when you know where something lives, or by tags when you don't.
